Why this design

  • Layer 6, not the last layer. A per-layer probe (notebook 04) is best in the middle of WavLM and weak at both ends; averaged over all test sets layer 6 is best (13.0% EER vs 17.2% for layer 12).
  • One-way attention. Only LFCC is updated. D3 (two-way) fits 2019 audio slightly better but generalises worse. The likely reason: LFCC carries recording and codec cues from the 2019 corpus that do not transfer, and keeping the WavLM tokens untouched stops those cues leaking into the WavLM view (not yet tested directly).
  • Small head. Only the projections, two attention blocks and the classifier are trained.
