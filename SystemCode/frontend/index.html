            Why LAYA/JEV Decision Policies Outperform Single Acoustic Detectors
          

          

            1. Selective Abstention (Option 1): 68.2% of acoustic classification errors concentrate within a narrow band around the operating threshold (|s - θ| < 3.0). By assigning ambiguous boundary samples to human forensic review, LAYA purifies the decided subset, lifting effective accuracy from 85.0% to 90.3% at 93% coverage.


            2. Cross-Modal Arbitration (Option 2): Self-supervised models (WavLM Base+) and spectral filterbanks (LFCC Cross-Attention) exhibit distinct blindspots, disagreeing on 10.3% of utterances. Normalizing scores into standard margins (z = (s-θ)/σ) enables LAYA to arbitrate conflicts autonomously, capturing a +5.00 pp accuracy improvement at 100% autonomous coverage.
